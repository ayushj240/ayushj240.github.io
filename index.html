Ayush Jain

I am a Research Scientist at Meta in the Applied Reinforcement Learning team. My research focuses on RL for general-purpose agents, with an emphasis on long-horizon decision-making in large, high-dimensional, changing, or otherwise complex action spaces. My experience spans computer-use agents, LLM post-training, recommender systems, and robotics.

I completed my PhD at USC with Prof. Joseph J. Lim and Prof. Erdem Bıyık. Previously, I have worked and interned at Meta Reality Labs, Microsoft Research Montreal, Naver AI, and Samsung Research Korea. I received my undergraduate degree from IIT Delhi.
